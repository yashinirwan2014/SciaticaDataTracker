Learn the basics

What is sciatica?

Sciatica describes pain or other symptoms that travel along the sciatic nerve pathway, usually from the lower back or buttock down the back of one leg. Only a qualified healthcare professional can assess what is causing someone's symptoms.

Original simplified educational illustration. It is not to scale and must not be used to locate an injury.

Commonly reported symptoms

What might people notice?

  • Pain travelling from the lower back or buttock down one leg.
  • Burning, shooting or electric-like pain in the leg.
  • Tingling or a pins-and-needles feeling.
  • Numbness or weakness in part of the leg or foot.
  • Symptoms that may feel worse with movement, coughing or sneezing.

Symptoms vary. Similar symptoms can have other causes, so a symptom list cannot confirm sciatica.

Important safety information

When to get urgent medical help

Seek emergency medical care immediately if you have any of these warning signs, especially if they are new:

  • New difficulty starting or controlling urination, inability to pass urine, or loss of bladder or bowel control.
  • New numbness around the genitals, inner thighs, buttocks or the area that would touch a saddle.
  • Severe or worsening weakness or numbness in both legs, or rapidly worsening leg weakness.
  • Back or leg symptoms after a serious accident or injury.

Do not wait for this website's checklist or a routine appointment. Tell a trusted adult and go to the nearest emergency department or contact your local emergency service. If you are unsure whether a symptom is urgent, get medical advice now.

Arrange a prompt doctor appointment for new, persistent, worsening or activity-limiting back or leg symptoms, even if none of the emergency warning signs are present. A clinician can assess the cause.

About this application

Built with familiar concepts

This tracker is an application of CBSE Class 12 Informatics Practices concepts in its Python and MySQL version.

  • Python: variables, data types, input/output, conditions, loops, functions and exception handling.
  • MySQL: tables, primary keys, INSERT, SELECT, UPDATE, DELETE, WHERE, ORDER BY, COUNT, AVG and SUM.
  • Data handling: recording, validating, searching, sorting and summarising information.

The browser page uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript for its interface and local browser storage. The browser tracker and Python–MySQL program are separate and do not synchronise.

Privacy: Entries are stored in this browser on this device using local storage. They are not sent to a server. Clearing browser data may erase them, so export a CSV backup regularly. CSV files are not encrypted; keep exported files somewhere private. Importing a CSV with an existing date replaces that date's entry.

Health note: This is a personal record-keeping and educational tool, not a medical device. The symptom guide cannot diagnose or rule out sciatica. Seek medical assessment for symptoms and emergency care for warning signs.
