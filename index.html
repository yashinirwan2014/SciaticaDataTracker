01 / The idea

One entry at a time.

The aim is simple: keep daily notes in one organised place, then look back at the information without making assumptions about it.

Each record brings together a date, a pain rating, study hours and walking-break details. You can add a note, revisit an earlier date, or remove an entry. The program stores the information in a MySQL table rather than leaving it scattered across separate notes.

02 / The daily record

Just the useful details.

A deliberately small set of fields keeps the project easy to understand and use.

01 — WELLBEING

Pain rating

Record a daily rating from 0 to 10, alongside an optional note for context.

02 — STUDY

Study time

Enter the number of hours spent studying on that date.

03 — MOVEMENT

Walking breaks

Keep count of walking breaks and the total time spent walking.

A note on use

A record is not a diagnosis.

This is an educational record-keeping project, not a medical device. Its summaries describe only the entries saved by the user. They cannot diagnose a condition, recommend treatment, or establish that study time or walking breaks cause a change in pain. Please keep personal health records and database passwords private rather than committing them to a public repository.
